## Project log

### 1. Digital Asset and Access Register with Control Dashboard

**Folder:** `digital-asset-access-register`
**Area:** Security, access management, backup and continuity
**Tools:** Excel

**The problem.** Monthly IT reports focused on activities and hours, which told management very little about risk.

**What I built.** A register of critical digital assets and a set of ten control areas, feeding an executive dashboard with five headline measures. It shows ownership, independent access, MFA, backup success, recovery test results and unresolved high risk issues.

**Status.** Sample version published with anonymised data.
